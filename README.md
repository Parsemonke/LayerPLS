LayerPLS finds the players in your zone with `/who` and whispers them one at a time for a layer invite, stopping as soon as someone invites you. They don't need the addon. 


<img src="Media/Portrait.png" alt="Alt text" width="100" height="100">  


- Everyone: your whole zone
- Avoid Nearby: your zone, except your current subzone
- -5 / +5 Levels: players up to 5 levels below or above you

Leave your group, click a button and accept the invite. Then click Zone Refresh next to the party frame and choose Refresh Now before you leave the group.

Type `/lp` to show or hide the window, or `/lp options` for skins, small button mode and your whisper text.

LayerPLS can't see layers, so some invites won't move you. These are real whispers and people can report you, so each player is only asked once an hour by default.

WoW: Forever 1.60.1 only.
